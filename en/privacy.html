Privacy Policy & Security Statement

Last Updated: August 31, 2026

1. Data Processing Philosophy

NinoPad is built upon a local peer-to-peer Wi-Fi network architecture connecting iPhone/iPad and Mac. No user account registration or login is required. We never route your control commands, typed text, or screen captures through any external cloud server.

2. Locally Stored Data

Your custom keypad decks, pairing credentials, device nicknames, and app preferences remain strictly in your device's local application sandbox. The main app and iOS Home Screen widgets share connection status via a secure, sandboxed App Group container.

3. Purchase & Subscription Security

Pro subscriptions and lifetime purchases are processed exclusively via Apple StoreKit. NinoPad only verifies validated active receipt entitlements and never accesses or stores credit card numbers, billing addresses, or Apple ID credentials.

4. iCloud Cloud Sync

When explicitly enabled, your custom keypad configurations and wallpapers sync across your devices via your personal Apple iCloud Private Container. NinoPad never uses this data for advertising or tracking.

5. Local Network & Screen Streaming

NinoPad uses Apple's native Bonjour protocol to discover Mac companion instances on your local Wi-Fi. Control commands are sent peer-to-peer. Low-latency screen mirroring only functions after explicit macOS Screen Recording authorization and remains strictly local.

6. Permissions Usage

Mac Accessibility permission is used solely to dispatch keyboard shortcuts and mouse clicks. Screen Recording and Microphone permissions are requested only when using desktop mirroring or the wireless microphone, and can be revoked anytime in System Settings.

7. Third-Party Services

The official website is delivered via encrypted HTTPS, and Mac releases are distributed through GitHub Releases. NinoPad contains zero third-party advertising SDKs or tracking frameworks.

8. Contact & Feedback

If you have questions regarding this privacy statement, reach out via GitHub Issues.
