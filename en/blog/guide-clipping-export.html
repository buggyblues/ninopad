Precise Organization & Archiving: Filter Clippings by Type & Export in One Click

Filter snippets by category tags and batch export to Markdown files · ~5 min read

Capturing research quotes is only half the battle; organizing them into structured reference material is what makes knowledge actionable. NinoPad allows you to filter research clippings by tag and export them as clean Markdown files with source URLs intact.

Practice Tip: Tag your clippings with consistent types (Idea, Method, Evidence) as you capture them to make downstream filtering effortless.

1. Open the Clipping Library

Navigate to the "Research" layout on NinoPad and tap the "Clipping Folder" icon to inspect all saved quotes, notes, and citations.

2. Filter by Category Tags

Tap the filter tags along the top bar to display specific snippet types: Ideas, Methods, Evidence, or Personal Notes.

3. Preview Structured Metadata

Select any item to review its captured quote, personal reflection, author, and original website or PDF URL.

4. One-Click Batch Export to Markdown

Tap "Export Selected" or "Export All". NinoPad generates clean, formatted Markdown files with YAML frontmatter, ready to drop straight into Obsidian, Notion, or your knowledge base.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is the exported Markdown file empty?

Check your current filter settings. If a filter tag with zero matching clippings is selected, the export output will be blank. Switch back to 'All Clippings' or verify tag counts before exporting.

Can I export clippings directly to Obsidian or Notion?

Yes. NinoPad exports standard Markdown (.md) files compatible with Obsidian, Logseq, Notion, and Bear, preserving frontmatter tags and source links.
