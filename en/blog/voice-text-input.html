Dictate on iPhone and Send Text to Mac

Dictate the draft first, proofread it on your phone, and then securely send it to your Mac · Read about 5 minutes

When writing long articles, replying to messages, or organizing meeting minutes, voice input directly on the computer is prone to input errors due to environmental noise or homophone recognition errors. NinoPad adopts the design concept of "first draft on the mobile phone, proofread it correctly, and then send it with one click", taking into account both speed and accuracy.

Practice Tips:First switch to draft mode, then dictate, revise, and finally input to Mac. Place the cursor in the text on your computer before sending.

1. The difference between text draft mode and real-time keystrokes

  • Text mode: Edit the draft first:Designed specifically for long paragraphs and oral shorthand. The text is only temporarily stored locally on the mobile phone. After clicking the "Up Screen" button, the entire text is delivered to the current focus cursor on the Mac at one time to avoid disturbing the computer cursor while recognizing it.
  • Live mode: Transfer to Mac as you type:Designed specifically for terminal commands and shortcut keys, every keystroke (such as Ctrl+C, Tab, Esc, direction keys) to trigger.

2. The four-step process of voice shorthand and proofreading

  1. Turn on text mode:Enter the input panel from the "Live Keyboard" on the work desktop and click "Text" at the top. The app will remember the last mode used. Make sure "Text" is selected before typing in the draft area.
  2. Tap the microphone to start dictating:Click the "Microphone" button at the bottom of the interface to call the iOS system's speech recognition engine to transcribe the spoken content into text in real time (networking and offline processing of speech recognition depend on the iOS system version and language settings).
  3. Check and modify on mobile phone:Click the microphone again to stop recording. Correct typos, fine-tune segmentation and punctuation directly on the touch screen of your phone.
  4. Enter to Mac:Place the cursor in the text of the Mac test document, and then click the upward arrow ("up screen") on the mobile phone input panel. Check that text appears where expected; it only handles typing and does not choose chat partners or submit messages for you.

3. Frequently used copy loading and one-click clearing

Click "History" in the input panel to retrieve the previous text and fill it back into the draft, and then display it on the screen after modification. Before clearing the draft, make sure that the required content has been retained to avoid clearing out unused text.
