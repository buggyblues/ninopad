Getting Started with Workplace Assistant: Make your iPhone a powerful companion next to your Mac keyboard

Quickly get started with the multi-functional work desktop: use the trackpad to navigate freely, use the real-time keyboard to type smoothly, and cooperate with undo, redo and one-click cutting and pasting to comprehensively speed up your desktop daily life.

September 10, 2026

Place your phone flat next to your Mac keyboard, and the work layout becomes your exclusive physical sub-console. Whether it's pointer fine-tuning, scroll wheel scrolling, or high-frequency copy, paste, and undo operations, it's all at your fingertips. Follow this basic guide to easily start an efficient dual-screen collaboration experience.

Picture description:The accompanying pictures come from the actual operation interface of the App, Mac connection and document content usage sample data. As you follow along, check the results of typing, undoing, and pasting in your own Mac documents.

Before starting

  • Install Naimao Control 1.2.0 on iPhone/iPad, and install the companion version of the same version on Mac; complete the pairing, and allow accessibility permissions on the Mac side. When you need the computer screen, turn on the screen recording permission.
  • Open a blank document in TextEdit on Mac and click on the text to make the insertion cursor visible. Don't practice on the password box, file name, or chat send box yet.

1. Open "Work" and confirm the operation goal

Click "Layout" at the bottom of the app and find "Jobs" in the list. When there are many layouts, use the search box to search for the name; if there are multiple desktops, click the arrow on the right to expand it, and then click the desktop name; you can also find it by searching for the specific desktop name.

There's a trackpad, mouse wheel, live keyboard, and keys for copy, paste, undo, redo, and more. Use the trackpad to move the Mac cursor and click back to the blank document; insert the cursor after you can see the text before continuing. The small demo screen at the top is no substitute for inspecting the actual window.

2. Click "Live Keyboard" and enter a line of practice text

Click "Live Keyboard" on the work desktop to open the input panel, and then click "Live" above the panel. The app remembers the last mode used, so it checks the selection first each time. The real-time mode will send text to the Mac as the keyboard is entered; first enter the short sentence "Write an outline first today" and observe whether the same content appears in the Mac document.

When inputting Chinese, complete the candidate word selection first, and then check the words in the document. If the text falls into other windows, stop typing first and re-click the text on your Mac. Do not send it continuously.

3. Return to the work desktop and try to undo it once

Click "Done" on the input panel, return to the work desktop, and then click "Undo". This key press sends Command-Z, and the actual undo unit is determined by the text editor: it may undo one word at a time, or it may undo a block of input.

After seeing clearly what just disappeared, click "Redo" once. Redo Send Shift-Command-Z; don't take this to mean that all software has the same operating history.

4. Practice copying and pasting

Select the sentence just now in the Mac document, return to the phone and click "Copy". Then start a new line on your Mac, make sure the selection is deselected and the insertion point is in place, and then click "Paste" on your phone.

Check if the new line is the same text. "Copy" and "Paste" correspond to Mac's Command-C and Command-V respectively, and use the Mac clipboard; they will not automatically put text into the phone's clipboard.

5. Find the editing entrance of the current desktop

When you need to adjust these keys, click "Layout" at the bottom, and then click "Edit Current Desktop". After the page enters the layout editor, first observe the position of each component; this exercise does not require moving or deleting any components. Click "Finish" to return to the control desktop.

If you want to add a frequently used button in the future, you can start here without having to create another working layout.

FAQ

The cursor moves, but the text is not entered into the document?

A normal mouse connection does not mean that the text focus is correct. First click on the editable text on Mac and confirm that the insertion cursor appears; then enter only one word to test and check the accessibility authorization.

Why doesn’t this text appear on my phone after copying?

The work layout's copy key operates the Mac's clipboard. To organize selected text on your phone, use the clipping process in the survey layout.
