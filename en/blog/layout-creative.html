3D Modeling Palm Companion: Blender Viewport Navigation & Shortcuts

Blender viewport navigation, mesh selection, and landscape two-handed ergonomics · ~5 min read

Complex 3D modeling software like Blender relies heavily on number pads and extensive multi-key shortcuts. NinoPad's Creative layout puts 3D viewport camera angles, object selection, and mode toggles within thumb reach.

Blender Tip: Hold your phone horizontally beside your drawing tablet or mouse for effortless viewport navigation with your non-dominant hand.

1. Launch Blender & Enter Studio View

Open Blender on your Mac. NinoPad automatically switches to the Blender preset deck with dedicated viewport and transform shortcuts.

2. Viewport Orbiting & Camera Jumps

Tap Top, Front, Right, and Camera view buttons to align your 3D perspective instantly, replacing the need for a physical numeric keypad.

3. Selection, Focus & Frame Selected

Tap "Select All (A)", "Focus Selected (Numpad .)", and "Toggle X-Ray" to navigate complex scenes with fluid hand movements.

4. Switch to Edit Mode & Sculpting Tools

Toggle between Object Mode and Edit Mode with a single tap, and trigger Extrude (E), Inset (I), and Bevel (Ctrl+B) operations cleanly.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does NinoPad recognize Blender automatically?

When Studio View is active, NinoPad monitors the frontmost Mac application and loads the corresponding shortcut map automatically.

Can I customize the viewport navigation dial sensitivity?

Yes. In the knob property panel, adjust the acceleration multiplier to achieve either pixel-precise micro-rotation or fast 360-degree sweeps.
