Make good excerpts of information: classify the original text, personal reflections and source links into categories

Create structured excerpt entries in the clipboard, clearly splitting the text, notes, and source URLs, so that you can accurately retrieve them in the future using only fragmentary memorized words.

September 10, 2026

A truly useful database will not confuse the original text with your own ideas. NinoPad Clipboard separates the main text, personal notes and original source links for you independently, allowing you to easily write down an action plan when excerpting, and instantly locate the original material at any time through note keywords.

Picture description:The text is practice material for the preparation of this article and is not quoted from the research paper. The accompanying images are actually added, edited and searched through the App; the clipping operation is saved locally and does not require the simulated Mac state to be regarded as the real source.

Before starting

  • Open the study layout and click "Clip" to enter the list; you can also refer to the study layout tutorial to find the entry.
  • Prepare a text that is allowed to be saved, as well as its actual corresponding source title and link.
  • The source link of the exercises in this article points to the NinoPad Magic Control Study Tutorial; the exercise sentences themselves are not quotes from the original text of the page.

1. Create a new collection and fill in the excerpt

Click "New Collection" in the upper right corner of the Clips main interface. In the text area, enter "Tutorial exercise: first check the original text, and then write your own summary."

The main text is suitable for excerpts, and your own understanding and to-do items are left in the notes area, making it easier to distinguish when looking back. The top of the page also provides entrances for photo albums, files, and pasting; this time, save the plain text first, and then add attachments according to the data type after you are familiar with it.

Reference:Study the clipping entry in the layout。

2. Open your notes and write down your thoughts

Click "Notes" to expand the input area and write an action memo for the material, such as "Friday review: Check whether the source link is complete."

Notes do not need to mechanically repeat the main text. You can write specific next steps such as "check the page number before quoting" and "compare with the previous version of the plan." Each note has a length limit. If the page prompts that the length exceeds the limit, please refine and streamline it before saving. Take another look before saving: keep excerpts of the text and your own thoughts on the notes, which will be easier to distinguish when exporting later.

3. Fill in the source and save the information

Scroll down and expand the "Sources" section. Fill in the source title as "NinoPad Magic Control Study Tutorial" and fill in the source link as https://ninopad.com/blog/layout-research.

Real material should be replaced with the actual page address, and you cannot just write the search engine homepage or complete the title based on impressions. Click "Save" in the upper right corner, and a "Tutorial Exercise" entry containing a summary of the notes should appear after returning to the list.

4. Open details and view saved content

Open the entry you just saved. The details page presents the main text and "notes" separately, and the source area clearly displays source information; clicking on a valid web link can jump directly back to the original source in the browser.

Check that the text is not mixed with your own additions, that the notes explain what to do next time, and that source titles and links point to the same material. If the excerpts come from multiple pages, it is easier to retain the source by creating separate entries, and then export them together.

5. If you need to add something, return to the editing page

Click "Edit" on the details page to modify existing text, notes or sources, and click "Save" when finished. Existing notes and sources will be expanded on the editing page, making it easier to add analysis and conclusions.

In this example, you can retain "Friday review" as a fixed search term, and then add specific inspection results. Open the original link to verify before modifying the source. Do not fill in unverified information just to make the entry appear complete.

6. Retrieve this clip using the words in the note

Return to the Clips list and enter "Friday Review" in the search box at the top. Even if there are no these words in the text, the system will present the entry through the note content index.

If not found, first switch the scope back to "All", switch the content type back to "All Types" and then try the keyword again. Scope, content type and search will work together to narrow the results; when troubleshooting, relax the conditions one by one to distinguish whether the entry does not exist or is hidden by filtering.

FAQ

Will manually creating a new one automatically know where I copied it from?

Don't rely on this. Manually practice filling in the source title and link by page, and then check it on the details page; only confirmed source information is suitable as a basis for future citations.

Why can’t I save notes after writing them?

First confirm that the text or attachment contains at least one piece of content, and no attachment is being imported; then check whether the note exceeds the length limit of the page prompt. If you only fill in notes but leave the text and attachments empty, they cannot be saved as empty content.

Can these items be brought to the computer together?

You can select multiple pieces of content in the clipboard and export them, save them to files through the system sharing panel or select available receiving methods. See the next tutorial for the export format and selection range.
