Easily control your speech: wireless remote control for page turning, picture-in-picture floating window and precise segment timing

It is equipped with forward and backward page turning and picture-in-picture camera/screen projection floating window, and uses segmented timers to accurately control the rhythm of rehearsal and speech, and calmly control each podium.

September 10, 2026

When giving a speech on stage or reporting remotely, the mobile phone is not only a flexible page turner, but also an all-round main control center. You can freely configure the position of the portrait camera and mobile phone screen projection window, and use the segmented timer that can be recorded to help you calmly control the rhythm of the entire presentation in every presentation.

Picture description:Camera settings, screen casting settings and timers are all operated in the App and screenshots taken. Mac connection uses sample state; actual capture and casting needs to be started, checked, and ended on your device.

Before starting

  • Install Naimao Control 1.2.0 on iPhone/iPad, and install the companion version of the same version on Mac; complete the pairing, and allow accessibility permissions on the Mac side. When you need the computer screen, turn on the screen recording permission.
  • Prepare a disposable presentation file. To use a camera or mobile phone to mirror the screen, follow the actual system prompts to grant the corresponding permissions; you only need to adjust the floating window settings without starting collection.

1. Enter the lecture assistant desktop and verify remote page turning.

Click "Layout" at the bottom of the iPhone, expand the presentation plan and select the "Lecture Assistance" desktop. The desktop is neatly arranged with core control keys such as previous page, next page, iPhone camera, and mobile screen projection.

Open a Keynote or PowerPoint presentation on your Mac and enter full-screen mode. Click "Next Page" on your phone to confirm on the Mac screen that the slide has successfully advanced to the next slide; click "Previous Page" to go back.

2. Long press the camera button to customize the picture-in-picture floating window on Mac

Press and hold the iPhone Camera button for about a second and when you let go, you should be taken to Camera Settings. Click "Upper Left", and then drag the "Floating Window Size" slider; click "Round Corners" if you need rounded corners.

Check the position preview and size changes. The mirror image only affects the camera display direction. Whether to turn it on or not depends on the effect you want the audience to see. Set the completion point to "Done" and return to speech assistance.

3. Independently configure the position of the floating window for mobile screen projection

Long press the "Mobile Screen Mirroring" button to enter the independent screen mirroring floating window settings page. The mobile screen projection and camera floating windows have their own independent sizes and docking position settings, so they will not overlap or interfere with each other.

Select a corner of the screen (such as the lower right corner) that does not block the core text and chart data of the slide. After adjusting the size, click "Finish" to exit the settings.

4. Practical demonstration: turn on the camera and screen projection with one click

After adjustment, short press the camera entry to enter the camera process; short press the mobile screen projection entry to enter the screen casting process. After completing the startup according to the system interface, you must confirm on Mac that the screen appears, is in the appropriate position, and has the correct content.

Start by using a page without sensitive content. When finished, use the corresponding shutdown or system stop broadcast entry; returning to the settings page does not mean that the system broadcast has been stopped.

5. Switch to speech timing and conduct segmented rhythm rehearsal

Click "Layout" in the bottom bar, search and switch to the "Speech Timing" desktop. Find the timer component and click "Start" at the beginning of the speech; whenever you finish a chapter or key point, tap "Pick" to record the time spent in each segment.

The "pointing" operation will leave a clear record of segment time and will not interrupt the overall total time at all; click "Pause" after the rehearsal to review the time distribution of each part. If you want to start a new round of rehearsal, click "Reset".

6. Complete the simulation throughout the entire process and confidently control the pace of the scene

Before officially going on stage, it is recommended to conduct a complete rehearsal according to the real process: open the screening, verify the front and rear page turning, call out the required portrait pop-up window, start the rehearsal timer and record it. Each step is verified with feedback on the Mac screen and mobile phone.

If you only need basic page turning for a certain speech, you can use the speech auxiliary desktop alone. There is no need to force the camera or screen projection function, and it will be more comfortable to travel lightly.

FAQ

Why does long pressing directly open the camera?

Use long press for setting and short press for activation. Press and hold for about one second before letting go, and make sure the title you enter is "Camera Settings"; do not make an additional click after long pressing.

The timer changes but no screen appears on your Mac?

Timing and image capture are different functions. The timer running does not mean that the camera or screen mirroring has started. You still need to check the system acquisition status and the Mac receiving screen.
