Presentation Master: Wireless Slide Remote, PiP Overlay & Split Timer

Wireless slide remote, floating camera overlays, and split rehearsal timers · ~5 min read

Step away from your laptop and command the room. NinoPad's Presentation layout equips you with a tactile slide remote, instant black-screen button, webcam Picture-in-Picture overlay, and multi-stage rehearsal timer.

Stage Tip: Test your wireless connection in the venue before presenting. Use the large tactile buttons to advance slides without looking down.

1. Blind-Touch Wireless Slide Clicker

Expansive Next and Previous touch zones let you advance slides smoothly while keeping your eyes on the audience.

2. Instant Black Screen & Stage Management

Tap the "Black Screen" button (B) to blank the presentation projector when you want full audience attention during live discussions.

3. Webcam Picture-in-Picture Overlay

Long-press to set floating camera coordinates, then tap to project a clean portrait video feed over your slides during virtual webinars.

4. Rehearsal Split Timer & Pace Tracking

Track intro, main pitch, demo, and Q&A section split times with vibrational milestone alerts that keep you perfectly on schedule.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does the slide clicker work when Mac Keynote is in full-screen presentation mode?

Yes. NinoPad sends system-level presentation events that advance slides seamlessly without needing active mouse focus.

Can I see my presentation speaker notes on the phone?

Yes. In the Presentation Notes desktop, view synced slide speaker notes and section milestones directly on your device.
