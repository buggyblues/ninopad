Control Mac Presentations from iPhone

Practical combat of flipping slides forward and backward, camera floating window, mobile screen projection and segment timing · Read about 5 minutes

When sharing in an online meeting or speaking on an offline podium, the presentation layout integrates wireless slide flipping, segment timers, portrait camera floating windows, and mobile phone screen projection functions, allowing you to get rid of the constraints of podium cables and freely control the rhythm of your speech.

Practice Tips:First try turning the page in the actual screening, and then turn on the camera, screen projection or timing as needed. Long press configuration and short press startup are different operations.

1. Wireless slide page turning and black screen control

When showing Keynote or PowerPoint in full screen on the Mac, the mobile phone has large forward and backward buttons, allowing you to turn pages blindly by holding it with one hand. When you need to pause the slideshow and draw the audience's attention back to the speaker, press the "Black Screen" button to temporarily black out the projection screen.

2. Portrait camera floating window

When giving an online presentation or recording a screen, press and hold the "iPhone Camera" button to set the position and size of the floating window. Click the button to superimpose a clear portrait camera picture-in-picture floating window on the Mac screen.

3. Mobile phone screen projection demonstration

When you need to display the mobile app, first press and hold "Mobile Screen Mirroring" to set the position and size of the floating window. Return to the desktop and short-press to start, then press the system prompt to start the screen broadcast, and confirm the received screen on your Mac. At the end, use the corresponding close or system stop broadcast portal. Returning to the settings page does not mean that the broadcast has stopped.

4. Segment timing and rehearsal management

Find the "Lecture Timing" desktop in the "Layout" list and click"Start". After finishing a part of the lecture, click"dot", continue to the next part; click "Pause" at the end of the rehearsal to view the accumulated time and segment records. When you need to re-time, confirm that the old records are no longer needed, and then click "Reset".
