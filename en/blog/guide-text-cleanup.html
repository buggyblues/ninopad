Standardize messy product data: Use SUBSTITUTE and TRIM to easily clean text

Visually configure text replacement and space removal functions on your mobile phone, distinguish between global replacement and specified first matches, and safely clean table data with the control column.

September 10, 2026

Got a product listing with redundant prefixes and random spaces? No need to manually delete line by line. Through the visual configuration of SUBSTITUTE on the mobile phone, specific marks are eliminated, and combined with the TRIM specification of continuous spaces, a clear comparison is established in two steps, and large-volume table text cleaning can be easily accomplished.

Picture description:The screenshots are actual operations in the App's native equation editor, using homemade trade names to practice. The results in this article are used to check formula semantics; there is no claim that the mobile phone has read these cells from Excel or automatically verified the entire table.

Before starting

  • Open the CSV of this article in Mac Excel and confirm that the Original is located in A1 and the three product names are located in A2:A4.
  • Enter Removed in B1 and Cleaned in C1, leaving column A as the original data.
  • Search for "Text Processing" in the layout list of Naimao Magic Control to enter the desktop corresponding to financial formulas.

1. Download the exercise sheet and keep the original data

Prepare test data in Excel or Numbers: Cell A2 has extra spaces before, after, and in the middle SKU-Blue Mug , A3 is "SKU-Red Cup", A4 is "SKU-Gift-SKU-Box" containing repeated prefixes. Never manually remove spaces first after opening a file.

A2 is used to test the effect of space regularization, and A4 is used to distinguish between "replace all matches" and "replace only the first match". Always keep the original column A data intact so that you can clearly verify whether the cleaning step will accidentally delete the legal characters inside the product name.

Download:Product name cleaning exercise data (CSV)。

2. Long press SUBSTITUTE to remove SKU-

In the mobile financial or text processing desktop, long press the "SUBSTITUTE" button to open the parameter panel. Fill in the "original text or cell" as A2(The type remains "Numeric/Reference"); "Search text" is filled in as "SKU-"(Type remains "literal"). "Replace with (leave blank to delete)" remains blank, and "Replace at which position" also remains blank for the time being.

At this point the bottom formula preview should be TRIM. Among them =TRIM(SUBSTITUTE(A2, "SKU-", "")) Indicates that the matched text is replaced with an empty string (that is, deleted directly). This is a legal null value setting. Do not fill in the blanks by mistake in order to make the input box "contained".

3. Place the formula in column B and view the replacement result

Enter the generated formula into B2 of Mac spreadsheet and press Enter. You can paste when the clipboard is shared at both ends, or you can connect the Mac and select B2 and click "Send Formula"; you can enter it manually when the clipboard is not shared. Later used in B3 =SUBSTITUTE(A3,"SKU-",""), used in B4 =SUBSTITUTE(A4,"SKU-","")。

Check column B results: B2 output Blue Mug (The prefix has been removed, but the original extra spaces are intact), B3 is "Red Cup", and B4 is "Gift-Box". After confirming that the first step of prefix removal is complete, proceed to the second step of space cleaning.

4. Use TRIM to sort out the spaces in column B

Return to the mobile text processing desktop, long press the "TRIM" button to open the panel, and fill in the parameters as B2, the type remains "numeric/reference". The bottom formula preview is =TRIM(B2), enter the formula into cell C2 of Mac as in the previous step, and then press Enter to confirm.

View the calculation result of cell C2: it is accurately rendered as "Blue Mug", the excess spaces at the beginning and end are completely removed, and multiple consecutive spaces between Blue and Mug are automatically compressed into a single standard space. Then apply it to C3 and C4 respectively. =TRIM(B3) with =TRIM(B4), the verification result is "Red Cup" and "Gift-Box".

Reference:Microsoft: Normal space range for TRIM。

5. If you only want to replace the first position, fill in 1

Press and hold SUBSTITUTE again to open the panel, still using A2 and SKU-, keep the new text blank, but fill in the number in the "Replace at which position" input box 1. The bottom formula preview becomes =SUBSTITUTE(A2,"SKU-","",1), meaning only the first occurrence will be replaced.

This parameter is particularly critical in cell A4: test in a blank cell =SUBSTITUTE(A4,"SKU-","",1), the expected result is "Gift-SKU-Box"; when the last 1 is omitted, all matches will be cleared and output to "Gift-Box". Choose flexibly according to the actual product number specifications, and do not blindly cover the entire quantity without verification.

Reference:Microsoft: Degree parameter of SUBSTITUTE。

6. Compare the results of the three columns and then process your own data

Review the three-column structure: column A is the original data that cannot be modified, column B shows the prefix stripping result, and column C shows the final result after space normalization. Before processing massive data, first extract representative sample rows (including no prefix, multiple prefixes, and special spaces) for verification to confirm that these rules are suitable for the current data.

If placeholders that look like spaces remain after cleaning the data copied from the web page, please identify the character encoding first. TRIM handles standard ASCII spaces and cannot directly remove non-newline spaces (NBSP, CHAR(160)); when encountering such special characters, you can replace them with SUBSTITUTE first and then TRIM.

FAQ

Why can I still copy the formula if I leave the new text blank?

The new literal of SUBSTITUTE is allowed to be an empty string, and the App will generate "". This means removing the matching content, which is a valid formula.

Why is A2 treated as two characters and the cell is not read?

Check whether the original text parameter is switched to "text". When referencing cells, select "Number, reference, or formula" and make sure the preview shows A2 instead of "A2".

Why is the SKU- in the middle of the name missing?

Omitting the count parameter will replace all matches. If you only want to replace the first time, fill in 1 in "Where to replace" and confirm with the practice line containing repeated matching. This is still a matching position rule, not a function that specifically determines prefixes.

Can I see the cleaned cells directly on my phone?

The formula editor in this article displays the generated formula text. The results should be confirmed in Excel and the formula preview cannot be read back as a cell.
