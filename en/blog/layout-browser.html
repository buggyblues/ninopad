Web browsing tool: Go directly to the website on your mobile phone and switch tabs with a swipe

Quickly enter and reach the target URL through the mobile phone, use the slider control to switch tabs freely, and combine on-page search with automated linkage to create a smooth surfing flow.

September 10, 2026

When checking information and comparing information, frequent switching between the keyboard and mouse often interrupts the train of thought. The browser layout integrates direct URL access, multi-tab switching, and in-page search on the mobile phone screen. You can switch pages with a simple swipe, and even supports automatically calling up the specified browser when switching layouts.

Picture description:The accompanying picture demonstrates the URL input and browser settings on the mobile phone, and the Mac connection uses sample data. Whether the URL is opened and tabs are switched, please confirm in the paired Mac browser.

Before starting

  • Install Naimao Control 1.2.0 on iPhone/iPad, and install the companion version of the same version on Mac; complete the pairing, and allow accessibility permissions on the Mac side. When you need the computer screen, turn on the screen recording permission.
  • Prepare a browser window on your Mac. The following explanation is based on the default shortcut keys; if you have modified the browser shortcut keys, restore or compare your own settings first.

1. Enter the browser layout and invoke the default browser

Click the "Layouts" tab at the bottom of your iPhone, find and enter the "Browser" layout in the list. When there are many list items, you can quickly search through the search box at the top.

After entering the layout, the App will automatically bring up the Mac's default browser to the foreground according to the preset action; you can also click the "Launch Browser" button on your phone at any time to actively send an activation request. The Mac default browser is taken from the macOS system settings.

2. Enter and reach the target URL directly with one click

Click "Enter URL or Search" on your phone and enter the target test URL in the pop-up text input panel, such as https://ninopad.com. After checking that everything is correct, click "Up Screen".

The key has a coherent sequence of automated actions built into it: it automatically focuses the Mac browser's address bar, then fills in the full URL, and finally automatically sends Enter. Confirm on your Mac screen that the web page has started loading without having to manually hit Enter repeatedly at the end of the text.

3. Create a new tab and experience the switching and closing of the slider.

Click "New Tab" on your phone, confirm that a new blank tab has been created in the Mac browser, and then use the method in the previous step to open another reference webpage. Swipe the Tabs slider on your phone left or right and watch the Mac browser smoothly switch between adjacent tabs.

Tap the slider control to quickly refresh the current page. Long pressing will trigger the closing of the current tab. If the closed page contains unsaved form data, please confirm the system pop-up prompt on the Mac before proceeding. If you close it by mistake, you can click "Restore Tab" to retrieve it again.

4. Quickly locate keywords in the text of the page

Click the "Search on page" button on your phone, and the Mac browser will automatically bring up the text search bar (⌘F). Then click "Keyboard Input" and enter the words that actually exist on the current web page (such as "layout").

Observe matching keyword highlighting positioning feedback on your Mac screen. This operation is used to quickly locate specific information on the current page; if you need to search the entire network, please return to "Enter URL or search" and enter the search term directly.

5. Check and configure the linkage actions after entering the layout

Click "Layout" in the bottom bar to enter solution management, expand the menu next to the browser solution and select "Post-switch action". The panel provides modes such as "Don't execute", "Default browser" and "Specified App".

First select "Default Browser" and save it to experience the linkage effect of automatically opening or activating the default browser on Mac every time you switch to this layout.

6. Advanced: bind specific third-party browsers

If you are more accustomed to using a specific third-party browser (such as Chrome, Edge, or Arc) for your daily work, you can select "Specify App" in "Post-Switch Action" and click the target software from the Mac application list.

After saving the configuration, re-enter the browser layout and verify on the Mac whether the specified browser window is accurately opened. This setting only changes the startup target and does not change the browser's general shortcut key mapping rules.

FAQ

The address is correct, why was it entered into the web form?

Check whether the browser is in the foreground and whether the address bar focus shortcut Command-L has been modified or blocked. First manually confirm on your Mac that Command-L can enter the address bar, and then try again.

History or tab shortcuts different from my browser?

Presets rely on browser shortcuts. Different browsers and user-defined mappings may differ; check the corresponding button bindings through the layout editor.
