Mac commonly used software portable control: create your own handheld application launcher

Automatically synchronize the list of commonly used Mac software, arrange the desktop with eye-catching large icons, support long-press editing and random replacement, and experience window switching with one touch.

September 10, 2026

No need to rummage through the crowded Dock or Launchpad. The launcher layout turns high-frequency apps on the computer into clear and easy-to-recognize solid blocks on the phone, and you can go directly to the front desk with a tap of your fingertips. This article will help you start by adding Safari and master the layout and management skills of the handheld launcher.

Picture description:The system application icon and sample application directory are used for illustrations, and the process of adding buttons is completed within the app. When your Mac is connected, the list of apps is based on what's available on that computer.

Before starting

  • Install Naimao Control 1.2.0 on iPhone/iPad, and install the companion version of the same version on Mac; complete the pairing, and allow accessibility permissions on the Mac side. When you need the computer screen, turn on the screen recording permission.
  • Leave the Mac companion running and wait for your app list to sync. The screenshots only use the demo directories of system applications such as Finder, Safari, Notes, etc.; your list is subject to the actual installed software.

1. Enter the launcher and check the application name first

Click "Layout" at the bottom of the app and find "Launcher" in the list. When there are many layouts, use the search box to search for the name; if there are multiple desktops, click the arrow on the right to expand it, and then click the desktop name; you can also find it by searching for the specific desktop name.

Each big button represents a Mac App. The icons in the screenshot are from system applications and are not blank placeholders. First select an app that you can confirm directly on your Mac, such as Safari; short-press to send a request to open or activate the app, and then observe the Mac front window.

2. Long press a button to enter editing

Press and hold the existing application button in the launcher for about one second. After letting go, you will enter the editor and select the button. Confirm that the "Change App" and Add App entries appear on the page before proceeding to the next step.

Short press to start, long press to edit. If the app is just opened, it means that the operation is recognized as a short press; just go back to the button and press it again.

3. Open the App list and search for the software you want to add.

Click "Add App" in the upper left corner of the editor and find "Search App" in the operation panel. Enter the software name and click the required App from the application list connected to Mac; check the name after selecting it, and then click "Finish" in the upper right corner of the panel.

Shown here is the app catalog provided by the connected Mac, not the App Store. If the search fails, first confirm that the software is indeed installed on that Mac, and then check whether the directory has been synchronized; do not just fill in a guessed application ID.

4. Finish editing and verify the new button on your Mac

After returning to the editor from the app selector, check the new button's name and icon, then click Done to exit the editor. Short-press the new button to confirm that your Mac opens the selected software.

If the button is outside the current screen, check other desktops under Scheme. Launcher defaults are grouped by up to eight app buttons per desktop; custom arrangements of existing desktops will also affect where you see them.

5. When replacement is needed, use “Replace App”

Press and hold the existing button again, click "Change App", and then select another software from the list. This is editing the target of the button, and adding a new button is two different operations.

"Manually specify app" is used when the exact Bundle ID is known, such as com.apple.finder. After filling in, you still need to short press to verify. Similar application names do not prove that the binding is correct.

FAQ

Why is my launcher different from the app in the screenshot?

The first synchronization will use the Dock application information connected to the Mac, and you can edit it later. The demo directory is only used for screenshots and will not replace your real application directory.

I clicked the button but no new window appeared on my Mac?

Existing applications may only be activated, not necessarily create new windows. First check whether the Mac menu bar is switched to the application, and then check the installation status, connection status and button binding.
