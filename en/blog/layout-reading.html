In-depth reading assistance: use your mobile phone to flip through PDFs, jump directly to chapters and jump to page numbers accurately

Synchronously read document bookmarks and hierarchical directories, support keyword filtering of chapters, absolute page number jump and text search, making reading large documents easier.

September 10, 2026

When studying long PDFs or white papers on your computer, you can easily get lost by dragging the scroll bar frequently. The reading layout brings the document table of contents to your mobile phone screen. You can jump by chapter with a tap, and you can enter the page number instantly, making browsing documents as comfortable as reading a physical book.

Picture description:This time, I actually open the table of contents, search for "Chapter 2" and open the page jump pop-up window. The "Reading Test.pdf" and chapters in the screenshot are from the demo state; whether it can read your table of contents, page numbers and actual jump positions needs to be checked in the Mac reader.

Before starting

  • Install Naimao Control 1.2.0 on iPhone/iPad, and install the companion version of the same version on Mac; complete the pairing, and allow accessibility permissions on the Mac side. When you need the computer screen, turn on the screen recording permission.
  • Open a PDF of at least three pages with searchable text in Preview on Mac. Table of contents exercises also require that the file itself contains a bookmarked table of contents; a table of contents page that is only visually typeset may not necessarily be read as a chapter list.

1. Enter the reading layout and prepare the test document on Mac

Click the "Layouts" tab at the bottom of your iPhone, find and enter the "Reading" layout in the list. When there are many list items, you can quickly search through the search box at the top.

Use a PDF reader or ebook software on your Mac to open a reference document (such as a tutorial or specification white paper with a table of contents) that contains a fully bookmarked table of contents.

2. Test single page flipping, confirm response and reader focus

Click "Next Page" on the mobile reading desktop and observe whether the document in the Mac reader smoothly flips to the next page; then click "Previous Page" to return.

Confirm that page turning shortcuts on your phone and Mac Reader are responding accurately with a single tap. In continuous scrolling mode, please refer to the actual field of view presented by the reader.

3. Read the chapter table of contents and check the document hierarchy

Click the "Directory" button on your phone and wait for the phone to read and present the hierarchical directory list of the document. Check whether the chapter titles displayed in the list match the actual bookmarks of the Mac PDF.

If the reader does not open the table of contents readback interface to the system, the interface will give friendly prompts; the demonstration chapter in this article is used to verify the table of contents interaction logic, and does not mean that all documents without bookmarks can automatically generate a table of contents.

4. Search for the chapter title and click to jump.

Enter a chapter keyword (such as "Chapter 2") in the "Search Chapter" box at the top of the table of contents panel, and the list will filter out matching entries in real time.

Click on the target chapter, the table of contents panel will automatically collapse, and the document on the Mac screen will immediately jump to the start page of the corresponding chapter. The pop-up window on the mobile phone closes to indicate that the command has been issued. Please confirm on the Mac that the page has been reached.

5. Use "jump page number" to go directly to the specified page

Click the "More" menu on the reading desktop and select "Jump Page Number". Enter the target page number (for example, 3) in the pop-up number box and click "Go".

Check the current page number and text content of the document on Mac to confirm that the absolute page number jump is accurate. Note that the physical page number of the PDF file may include the cover page and the preface, and it is normal to have a slight deviation from the printed page number of the main text.

6. Switch to text search and locate document details in depth

The table of contents search filters only chapter names. To find words in the text, return to the reading desktop and click the search entry, enter the words that exist in the file in the "Find Text" pop-up window, and then check the matching results on Mac.

If a purely scanned image PDF does not undergo OCR text layer recognition, it may not be possible to directly retrieve the text; when you encounter the essential paragraphs, you can use the clipboard function to organize and archive them.

FAQ

There is no directory. Is the app not connected?

Not necessarily. The document may not have readable bookmarks, or the reader may not provide table of contents capabilities. Check page turning and text search first, and then check the table of contents of the document itself.

Why doesn’t my phone display the entire PDF?

This layout is the control interface for Mac Reader. It reads the available state and sends actions without automatically importing the entire book to the phone.

Are the buttons different after switching to EPUB?

Buttons vary according to reading type and software capabilities. Topics, chapters, bookmarks and other operations in books cannot be directly applied to the PDF page numbering process.
