Deep Reading Assistant: PDF Navigation, Table of Contents & Page Jumps

Sync document TOC, filter chapter titles, jump to page numbers, and search text · ~5 min read

Reading dense academic papers, textbooks, and technical manuals on a computer is fatiguing. NinoPad's Reading layout transforms your phone into a dedicated reading remote with dual-direction page knobs, interactive chapter tables of contents, and instant page jumping.

Reading Tip: Open your target PDF in Preview or Skim, then use NinoPad's rotary dial to turn pages comfortably with one hand.

1. Dual-Direction Page Knob & Smooth Scrolling

Turn the tactile rotary dial to flip pages smoothly or scroll long documents with hardware-like physical inertia.

2. Interactive Table of Contents & Chapter Filter

Browse the document's hierarchical outline on your phone. Filter chapters with keywords and tap to jump immediately on your Mac screen.

3. Exact Page Number Jumps

Enter any page number in the quick numpad dialog to bypass endless scrolling across large 800-page volumes.

4. Full-Text Search & Keyword Navigation

Search key terms directly from your mobile deck and step through matching occurrences in your Mac reader.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which Mac PDF and ebook readers are supported?

NinoPad integrates with Apple Preview, Apple Books, Skim, Adobe Acrobat Reader, and PDF Expert.

How does page number jumping work?

Tap 'Jump to Page', enter the target page number (e.g., 142) on mobile, and NinoPad navigates your Mac document immediately.
