Devices throughout the house are at your fingertips: connect to Home Assistant and light up your inspiration with physical knobs

Connect to Home Assistant through the service address and access token, add smart lamps to the mobile desktop, and experience smooth switching and stepless light and dark dimming.

September 10, 2026

Free common home controls from cumbersome app menus. By binding Home Assistant to your smart home layout, you can turn your desk lamp or room light into a knob component with delicate scales, and switch and adjust brightness at the palm of your hand.

Picture description:The device in the picture is provided by the local demo service, and switching operations will update the demo status. Once you're connected to a real home, check your phone's readback, Home Assistant status, and actual device action at the same time.

Before starting

  • Install Naimao Magic Control 1.2.0 on iPhone/iPad. This function can be used independently and does not require connection to a Mac.
  • Prepare Home Assistant that is already running, and a light that can be controlled normally on the platform. The phone needs to be able to access its address; first verify that the switch is working in Home Assistant itself.

1. Enter the smart home layout and click on the "Connect Home" portal

Click the "Layouts" tab at the bottom of your iPhone to find and enter the "Smart Home" layout in the list. For multi-desktop solutions, please expand the arrow on the right and select the corresponding desktop.

When entering for the first time and the connection has not been configured yet, the "Connect Home" button will be displayed in the center of the desktop. Click it to enter the Home Connect wizard and select Add Home Assistant service. If the device has been added before, you can manage it directly on the existing connection without having to build the network again.

2. Fill in the Home Assistant service address and long-term access token

Fill in your Home Assistant address in the "Service address" field of the connection form. For example, a local instance might use homeassistant.local:8123, but this must be replaced with an address that the phone can actually access.

Create a long-term access token on the Security page of your Home Assistant profile and copy it into the app's Access Token field. Do not fill in the login password as a token. If using local HTTP, follow the page prompts to confirm "Allow this local HTTP connection"; the token is only used for your own connection, do not include it in the tutorial screenshot.

Reference:Home Assistant official profile and security settings instructions。

3. After the connection is successful, add the smart lighting component to the desktop

Click "Connect" and wait for a clear connected status and device list. Click "Return to Layout, Add Component", then enter the editor from "Add Equipment Component" in the empty layout, select the home equipment component and bind the lamp just now.

You can also use the "Automatically add... online devices" entry on the empty desktop to join in batches. The successful connection and the component being added are two steps; just filling in the token will not allow each device to automatically appear on the existing desktop.

4. Check the device name and room, click the switch in the center of the knob

Save the desktop and return to the main interface, check the device name on the card and the room it belongs to (such as "desk lamp/study"). Tap the center area of ​​the light knob to send an on-off command, observe the status changes of the center indicator light, and confirm in the real room that the physical lamps have been turned off or on simultaneously.

The phone will wait for Home Assistant's real status readback and update the UI. The instantaneous animation of button press is only touch feedback. Please refer to the actual physical lamp or status readback.

5. Press and hold the outer ring of the knob to adjust the light brightness smoothly

The center of the light knob is responsible for turning the power on and off, and the outer ring is responsible for dimming. Press and hold the outer ring of the knob and slowly rotate it clockwise to increase the brightness, rotate it counterclockwise to decrease the brightness, and wait for the status value to be read back after letting go.

First make small adjustments to confirm that the light and dark changes of the physical lamp are consistent with the percentage value displayed on the mobile phone. The dimming function requires that the lamp hardware itself supports the dimming protocol. Ordinary on-off sockets only support switch operations.

6. Click the device name to view details and expand the smart ecosystem of the whole house

Click the device name area below the card to open the details and view the status and more controls provided by the device. Check here if you're offline or having an error, then go back to Home Assistant to check the device itself.

When the switch and readback of a light are verified, add curtains, sockets and other equipment, and check their true actions one by one. Matter devices must be added to Apple Home or Home Assistant first. This App does not provide an independent Matter network configuration process.

FAQ

The connection is successful, but there is no device on the desktop?

First check if Home Connection finds supported devices, then return to the layout to add and bind home device components. Existing connections will not automatically rearrange your previously edited desktop.

Why is the button grayed out when the status shows offline?

Offline devices are not guaranteed to receive control requests. First check the online status in the home platform and the connection from the mobile phone to the service address; after recovery, wait for the new status to be read back.

Can I switch to Apple Home?

You can connect to an existing Apple home and follow the system prompts to allow access. The device needs to be added and available on the home platform first; capabilities such as cameras and door locks are not supported by the lighting exercises in this article.
