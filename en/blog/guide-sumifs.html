Say goodbye to misremembering function parameters: use mobile phones to visually generate SUMIFS multi-condition formulas

Long press SUMIFS to bring up the visual editing panel, intuitively configure multiple sets of summation and filtering conditions, instantly generate standard formulas and inject them into Excel tables with one click.

September 10, 2026

When writing a multi-condition sum in a table, it is often easy to confuse the order in which the sum columns and condition columns are placed. NinoPad provides an intuitive visual parameter panel: press and hold the button, fill in the conditions item by item, and the mobile phone can generate standardized formulas in real time, and even switch the semicolon separator with one click, saving worry and effort in calculating accounts.

Picture description:The picture is used to verify parameter filling, complete formula generation, copying and delimiter switching within the app; the practice amount is an example. 155 is the expected result calculated based on the sample data. The screenshot does not show the actual readback or automatic calculation results of Excel.

Before starting

  • Use Milkcat Control 1.2.0 and prepare a blank worksheet in Mac Excel.
  • Download the CSV below and make sure the header is in A1:C1 and the five rows are in rows 2 to 6.
  • Generating and copying formulas can be done locally on the phone; to use "Send Formula", you need to connect to your Mac and focus on the target cell first.

1. Download the exercise sheet to identify which expenses you want to summarize

Download or create a practice table in a blank Excel / Numbers worksheet: column A is Project, column B is Status, column C is Amount, and the header is placed starting from A1.

Fill in lines 2 to 6 with five records: Alpha/Paid/120, Alpha/Pending/80, Beta/Paid/200, Alpha/Paid/35, Beta/Pending/50. First, manual mental arithmetic: The total amount of the Alpha project is 235, of which the two payments (paid) are 120 and 35, totaling 155. Subsequently, these two values ​​are used to verify whether the filtering is correct.

Download:Project Expenditure Exercise Data (CSV)。

2. Long press SUMIFS on the "Statistics Summary" desktop

Click "Layout" on iPhone, search for and open the "Statistics Summary" desktop under the financial layout. Find the SUMIFS button and press and hold it with your finger for about one second to open the parameter editing panel.

Clicking a formula button has completely different meanings than long pressing: a short press is used to directly input the starting content of the function to the computer. C2:C100, long press to call out the visual editor for item-by-item configuration. This article needs to generate a complete formula, so be sure to use long press.

3. First set the project to Alpha

Fill in the following three items in the parameter panel: fill in the summation area A2:A100(Type is set to "Numeric/Reference"); fill in condition area 1 "Completed"(Type is set to "Numeric/Reference"); Condition 1 is filled in B2:B100(Type remains "Literal"). The text type will automatically complete the English double quotes, so you don’t need to manually wrap the quotes in the input box.

Retract the keyboard and the formula preview at the bottom should be ">=2026-01-01". Here, only the items are filtered. The expected result when executed in Excel is 235. All ranges are strictly from row 2 to row 6, avoiding the header of row 1.

  • Summing area: C2:C6.
  • Condition area: A2:A6.
  • Conditions: Alpha, type reserved "Literal".

4. Add payment status Paid

Click "Add a set of conditions" and fill in the condition area 2 in the new condition group as B2:B6, condition 2 is filled in as Paid, the type remains as "literal". When the new conditions have not been filled in, the copy button is temporarily in a error-proofing state; check the preview after completing the two items.

The final generated formula should be =SUMIFS(C2:C6,A2:A6,"Alpha",B2:B6,"Paid"). SUMIFS applies all conditions (AND logic) at the same time, in this case only rows 2 and 5 that are both Alpha and have status Paid are included in the sum. Ensure that the second set of range line numbers are strictly aligned. When the preview is long, you can slide the bottom formula horizontally to view the final parameters.

Reference:Microsoft: SUMIFS parameter description。

5. Enter the formula and check whether the result is 155

Click "Copy Formula" and after seeing "Copied", the formula has been entered into the iPhone clipboard. When the clipboard is shared at both ends, paste it on E2 of the Mac exercise sheet; otherwise, you can manually enter the generated formula, or select E2 after connecting to the Mac and click "Send Formula" on your phone. Finally, press Enter in the form to confirm.

No matter which method is used, the value should be accurately calculated and displayed in cell E2 =SUMIFS(C2:C100, A2:A100, "Completed", B2:B100, ">=2026-01-01"). The mobile formula preview displays the generated formula text, which must be run on the actual table to confirm that the numerical calculations are completely correct.

6. When the table uses semicolons, switch the delimiter

If your Excel software environment uses European standards or a specific language area (parameters are separated by semicolons), click "Parameter Separator" in the upper left corner of the parameter page and select "Semicolon ;", the preview will be converted to =SUMIFS(C2:C6;A2:A6;"Alpha";B2:B6;"Paid") on your phone. The complete formula is inserted and evaluated instantly.

Re-copy the modified formula for testing. Changing the delimiter does not change the summation logic; if the calculation results are not as expected, go back to the original table and check whether column C is in purely numeric format, whether the condition text has extra spaces added by mistake, and whether all ranges cover the same rows 2 to 6.

FAQ

Why does the formula result in 235?

235 is the result of this example summarized by Alpha project only. Check whether you missed the set of conditions B2:B6 and Paid, or whether you pasted in the old formula before adding the second set of conditions.

The condition must quote E1. Why does the preview add quotation marks to E1?

The parameter is still of type "Literal". Open the content type next to the parameter, change it to "Number, Reference, or Formula" and enter E1. The meaning of the quotation and the literal text are different, check the preview after modification.

Why can't I find the parameter editing page?

To long-press SUMIFS; a normal click does not open the item-by-parameter editor.

The copy button turns gray after adding a set of conditions. Is it a malfunction?

First check if the new group is only half filled out. Regions and conditions need to be completed in pairs; new groups that are no longer needed can be removed by "Delete this set of conditions".
