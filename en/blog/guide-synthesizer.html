Synthesizer Starter: Diatonic Chords & Drum Patterns

Play lush chords, modulate filters with tactile knobs, and record multi-track loops · ~5 min read

Unleash your musical creativity anywhere. NinoPad's Synthesizer layout turns your phone into a standalone polyphonic music workstation with smart diatonic chord pads, resonant filter knobs, and a 6-track pattern looper.

Music Tip: Choose your root key (e.g., C Major / A Minor). Diatonic mode guarantees that every chord pad sounds harmonious together!

1. Play Harmonies with Smart Chord Pads

Tap the 8 diatonic chord pads (I, ii, iii, IV, V, vi, vii°, Oct) to trigger lush 4-voice polyphonic voicings with velocity dynamics.

2. Modulate Resonant Filters & Effects

Turn the rotary dials to sweep the Low-Pass Filter Cutoff, boost Resonance, and dial in warm Chorus or Space Reverb in real time.

3. Layer Drum Grooves with the Step Sequencer

Switch to the Drum panel, select kick, snare, and hi-hat sounds, and tap the 16-step grid to lay down infectious beats underneath your chord progression.

4. Record Multi-Track Looper Sessions

Hit the Record button to capture a 4-bar chord rhythm, overdub melody leads, and export the entire track as a lossless WAV or MIDI file.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use NinoPad's synthesizer without connecting to a Mac?

Yes! NinoPad has a built-in on-device DSP sound engine that produces rich stereo audio directly through your iPhone/iPad speakers or headphones.

How do I sync loop tempo (BPM) with external DAWs?

NinoPad supports Ableton Link and MIDI Clock synchronization over Wi-Fi for rock-solid tempo alignment with your Mac DAW.
