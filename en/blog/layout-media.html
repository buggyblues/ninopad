The classic click wheel returns: Use your phone to elegantly control Mac music and playback progress

Reproduce the classic click wheel and hierarchical menu feel, check the current track and cover at any time, smoothly fine-tune the playback progress and switch playback modes.

September 10, 2026

When listening to music for work, you can switch songs and adjust the volume without interrupting your focused work on the screen. The media layout reproduces the classic virtual click wheel interaction with the small screen. You can precisely fine-tune the song progress by sliding, and you can explore the playback mode by tapping MENU, bringing a ritualistic music remote control experience.

Picture description:The track "Afternoon Practice" in the accompanying picture is sample data, and the menu is from the actual App interface. For final results on playback, progress, and modes, please check against the Mac player you are using.

Before starting

  • Install Naimao Control 1.2.0 on iPhone/iPad, and install the companion version of the same version on Mac; complete the pairing, and allow accessibility permissions on the Mac side. When you need the computer screen, turn on the screen recording permission.
  • Manually play a song you have permission to play in the Music app on Mac. Check whether the corresponding song name is displayed on your phone; the "Afternoon Practice" in the screenshot of this article is a fictitious demo entry, not the real track that needs to be searched.

1. Enter the media layout and check the currently playing information

Click the "Layouts" tab at the bottom of your iPhone, find and enter the "Media" layout in the list. If there are many layouts, you can use the search bar at the top to quickly locate them.

After entering, first observe whether the song name, artist, playback progress and status displayed on the small screen of your mobile phone are consistent with the player running on your Mac. Press the play/pause key to confirm that the Mac sound is paused immediately, and press the key again to resume playback.

2. Press the MENU key to enter the small screen level menu

Click the MENU button above the roulette wheel, and the small screen of your phone will immediately switch from playback view to hierarchical menu mode. You can either click on the menu item directly on the screen, or slide the cursor along the virtual click wheel, and finally press the center confirmation key to enter the selected item.

First find "Now Playing". In other menu pages, MENU is used to return to the previous level; you can continue to view song information after returning to "Now Playing".

3. Configure playback mode and two-way status synchronization

Click "Play Mode" in the menu to view control options such as random play, single loop, list loop, etc. After selecting one of them, check whether the corresponding mode icon changes simultaneously in the Mac player interface.

The functions provided by different players (such as Apple Music and Spotify) are slightly different; for example, the function of collecting songs depends on the support of the specific player, and the interface options are subject to the actual playback ability.

4. Use the virtual scroll wheel to fine-tune progress

Return to the "Now Playing" interface and press the center confirmation key to enter the "Adjust Progress" mode. At this time, swipe the click wheel clockwise or counterclockwise to observe the changes in the time value on the small screen, and confirm on the Mac that the audio playback position has been accurately adjusted.

The center confirmation key can also rotate between views such as cover images and song details. Please read the page title clearly before operating the scroll wheel to avoid confusing volume adjustment with progress adjustment.

5. Return to daily playback to understand player permissions and restrictions.

After practicing, press the MENU key to return to the "Now Playing" main interface and retain the desired play mode. Functions such as playlist expansion, real-time lyrics display, and audio track output switching rely on the data channel opened by the Mac player to the system.

If a display is temporarily unavailable, first complete the same operation on your Mac to confirm that the player itself provides it. Just because a regular player can receive playback controls doesn't necessarily mean it can provide lyrics, collections, or a complete library.

FAQ

Your phone has a play button but no song title?

Play a song on your Mac first, then check your media sources and connections. Ordinary media control and track information reading are different capabilities and are not guaranteed to be available at the same time.

What to do if the lyrics or playlist is empty?

Do not use repeated clicks to infer that the read has been successful. First check the player support and song information; if the lyrics do not match, you can try again later. If the database is empty, check the content on Mac.
