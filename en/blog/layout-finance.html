Finance & Spreadsheets: Fast Calculation & Visual Formula Building

Independent on-device calculator, numeric keypad, and 64 formula assistants · ~5 min read

MacBook keyboards lack dedicated numeric keypads, making heavy financial accounting and data entry tedious. NinoPad's Finance layout provides a full tactile numeric keypad, local scratch calculator, and visual formula helpers.

Workflow Tip: Use the on-device calculator to compute numbers locally, then tap 'Send' to inject the final sum into your spreadsheet cell.

1. Dedicated Tactile Numeric Keypad

Enjoy large, comfortable number keys with standard arithmetic operators (+, -, *, /), parentheses, and decimal points for swift data entry.

2. On-Device Scratch Calculator

Calculate multi-line totals on your phone independently. The result stays visible on mobile without cluttering your desktop spreadsheet cells.

3. 64 Built-In Formula Assistants

Browse categorized formula templates for Financial, Statistical, Logical, and Text operations with interactive parameter wizards.

4. One-Tap Formula Injection

Configure complex expressions like SUMIFS, XLOOKUP, and NPV visually, then inject standard syntax straight into Excel or Numbers.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use the numeric keypad over Bluetooth or Wi-Fi?

NinoPad operates over local Wi-Fi with ultra-low latency, sending standard numpad scan codes recognized by Excel, Numbers, and Google Sheets.

How do I inject calculated totals directly into my Mac spreadsheet?

Perform your calculation on the phone, then tap 'Send Value' to inject the result directly into the selected Mac spreadsheet cell.
