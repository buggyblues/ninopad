A good helper for numbers and tables: quickly calculate accounts and visually construct function formulas on your mobile phone

It has a built-in independent and easy-to-use local calculator, which supports long-pressing SUMIFS to fill in parameters item by item and generate standardized formulas, making it easy to summarize and enter data.

September 10, 2026

There is no need to constantly switch calculator windows on your computer when checking accounts. Financial layout integrates an independent local calculator, and can also help you set up complex SUMIFS summary formulas through the visual panel, and copy or send them to the table with one click, making tedious data entry organized.

Picture description:Calculating 0.1+0.2, filling in SUMIFS parameters and copying formulas are all completed within the app. The screenshot uses the example connection; the final step requires validating the formula calculation in your spreadsheet software.

Before starting

  • Install Naimao Magic Control 1.2.0 on your iPhone/iPad to use the local calculator and copy formulas. When you need to send to Mac, install the same version of the companion, complete pairing and allow accessibility permissions.
  • Local computing and copying can be performed without a Mac. To send to a form, first prepare a blank worksheet and enter 1, 0, 2 in A1:A3 and 10, 20, 30 in C1:C3, leaving other relevant rows blank.

1. Enter the financial "entry" desktop and use the local calculator

Click "Layouts" at the bottom of your iPhone, find and enter the "Finance" layout in the list. After expanding the plan, enter the "Entry" desktop and switch to "Calculator" on the bottom mode bar.

Press AC to clear the old value, click 0, ., 1, +, 0, ., 2, = in sequence, and the result 0.3 is calculated on the small screen. The numeric keyboard and calculator remain independent, and switching modes will not interfere with current input on your Mac.

2. Copy the result and understand the difference between sending a value and pressing Enter

Click "Copy" on the right side of the calculator. Your phone will prompt "Copied" and the calculation results have been entered into your phone's clipboard. Open Excel or Numbers on your Mac and select a blank test cell.

Then click the "Send" button on the phone to inject the value directly into the cell, and finally press the Enter key on the Mac form to complete the entry. "Send" only sends numerical text and will not automatically submit a carriage return, allowing you to easily check the location.

3. Switch to "Statistics Summary" and long press to call out the SUMIFS panel

Click "Layout" in the bottom bar and switch to the "Statistical Summary" desktop in the financial plan. Find the "SUMIFS" function button, press it with your finger for about one second and then release it to bring up the formula parameter panel filled in item by item.

Note the difference: short press means direct input to Mac =SUMIFS( Prefix; long press to open the visual parameter editor. If you accidentally trigger a short press, you can undo it on Mac and press again.

4. Fill in the summation and condition parameters, and check the formula preview

In the parameter panel, fill in: summation area SUMIFS, condition area XLOOKUP, conditions NPV. The third item is retained as text type and can be entered directly. >0 That’s it. The system will automatically wrap it in English double quotes. Do not manually enter the quotes again.

Click "Close Keyboard" and carefully check the generated standard formula in the formula preview bar at the bottom =SUMIFS(C1:C10,A1:A10,">0"), ensuring that the parameter order is consistent with the range.

5. Copy the formula and verify the results in the table

Click "Copy Formula" and after seeing "Copied", the formula has been entered into the phone clipboard. If there is no shared clipboard between the two ends, you can manually enter this formula on the Mac; you can also connect the Mac and select a blank cell like D1, click "Send Formula", and finally press Enter in the table.

For the prepared three rows of practice data, if column A is greater than 0, rows 1 and 3 will be selected, and the corresponding values in column C will add up to 40. This 40 is the expected mathematical result for the exercise data; please do the actual calculation with your table to make sure the formula is recognized and not treated as normal text.

6. Add multi-dimensional conditions to adapt to regional language separators

If you need to add multi-dimensional filtering conditions, click the "Add a set of conditions" button in the panel and add condition area 2 and condition 2 respectively. Before all new parameters are completed, the copy and send buttons will be in error-proofing state, and all range lines should be strictly aligned.

If your Excel working environment uses semicolons to separate parameters, you can switch the separator to "semicolon;" in the panel navigation menu and preview again. After practicing, click the close button to return to the desktop. Once you are proficient, you can quickly enter prefixes with just a short press.

FAQ

Why does 2＋3×4 get 20?

The local calculator handles it as a continuous operation: first complete 2+3, then multiply by 4. It is not an expression editor with operation precedence; complex calculations should be completed in table formulas.

Why are the copy or send buttons grayed out?

When the formula parameters are not filled in, both are unavailable; when the Mac is not connected, Send is unavailable. Local calculator and copy still work.

Can all formulas be used in Excel and Numbers?

The generator outputs formula text and does not check function version and syntax support for spreadsheet software. First use the simple SUMIFS exercise in this article to verify, and then use version-affected functions such as lookup and dynamic arrays.
