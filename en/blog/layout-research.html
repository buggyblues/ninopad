Knowledge Extraction Workflow: Capture Quotes, Thoughts & Citations

Capture quotes from Mac selection, record personal reflections, and retain citations · ~5 min read

Synthesizing research from dozens of papers and articles requires capturing evidence without losing citations. NinoPad's Research layout establishes an organized pipeline from raw quotes to personal insights and exportable notes.

Research Method: Categorize every saved snippet into Idea, Method, or Evidence tags to streamline thesis writing later.

1. One-Tap Text Selection Capture

Highlight any text on your Mac and tap "Clip Selection" on NinoPad to grab the quote instantly.

2. Add Personal Commentary & Critical Thoughts

Expand the note editor on your phone to append your own analysis, questions, and cross-references while reading.

3. Auto-Preserve Source URLs & Citations

NinoPad automatically binds the active web URL or document filename to the snippet card for permanent reference traceability.

4. Filter, Tag, and Export to Markdown

Organize snippets with custom tags and batch export them as structured Markdown documents for Obsidian, Logseq, or Notion.

Frequently Asked Questions

How are clippings synced between mobile and Mac?

Clippings are stored locally on your device and can be exported as standard Markdown files or synced directly to Mac notes apps over Wi-Fi.

Can I attach PDF page numbers to clippings?

Yes. When clipping from supported Mac PDF readers, NinoPad automatically captures the current page number alongside the text.
