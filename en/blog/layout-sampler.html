Palm Groove Workstation: Sample Packs, Drum Slicing & Sound Design

16 responsive touch drum pads, sample sound kits, choke groups, and beat slicing · ~5 min read

Produce beats wherever inspiration strikes. NinoPad's Sampler layout brings the legendary 16-pad MPC drum machine workflow to your touchscreen with zero-latency audio triggering, sample slicing, and choke group cutoffs.

Groove Tip: Practice playing kick on Pad 1 and snare on Pad 2 with your thumb, layering hi-hat patterns with your index finger.

1. Load Built-In Sound Kits

Choose from vintage 808s, boombap acoustic drums, synthwave kicks, and vocal chops in the sound library.

2. Tap 16 Velocity-Responsive Pads

Perform dynamic drum grooves with responsive multi-touch pad triggers and satisfying haptic clicks.

3. Sample Fine-Tuning & Choke Groups

Adjust sample pitch, attack, decay, and assign mutual choke groups so open hats cut off realistically when closed hats trigger.

4. Import Custom Audio & Slice Beats

Drop your own WAV/MP3 loops onto pads, slice breakbeats automatically across 16 pads, and perform remixes live.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do choke groups work on the drum pads?

Assigning open and closed hi-hats to the same choke group ensures that tapping the closed hat immediately cuts off the ringing open hat sound, mimicking real drum kits.

Can I record my own audio samples via the mobile microphone?

Yes. Tap 'Record Sample' to capture field recordings, vocal chops, or acoustic percussion directly into any of the 16 pad slots.
