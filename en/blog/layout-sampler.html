Handheld Rhythm Workstation: Load sample drum beats, fine-tune sounds and explore slicing gameplay

Hit the touch drum pads on your mobile phone, explore the sampling menu and loudness arrangement, distinguish the rules of sound stacking and relay cutting, and start rhythm creation anytime and anywhere.

September 10, 2026

Capture sonic inspiration anytime, anywhere, without needing to connect to a computer. The sample layout has built-in sound libraries and responsive percussion pads that support volume fine-tuning, loudness optimization, and mutually exclusive group settings, allowing you to create dynamic beats at the tap of your fingertips.

Picture description:Screenshot from the app's native sampler, using playable synthesized sample drum sounds. This time, actually click on the drum pad and open the sound editing page; screenshots cannot prove the listening experience of the speakers. Please confirm on your own device when listening.

Before starting

  • Install Naimao Magic Control 1.2.0 on iPhone/iPad. You don't need a Mac for this exercise. Turn down the volume before starting to avoid hearing the sound the first time.
  • Use the built-in examples or audio you have access to. The kick, snare, hi-hat, and claps in the screenshots are synthesized samples, not user recordings.

1. Load samples from the sound library

Click "Layout" at the bottom of the app and find "Sampling" in the list. When there are many layouts, use the search box to search for the name; if there are multiple desktops, click the arrow on the right to expand it, and then click the desktop name; you can also find it by searching for the specific desktop name. Enter the "Record Sound" desktop. When there is no sound when using it for the first time, click "Sound Library", click "Try Sample Sounds" in the empty list, wait for the loading to complete, and click "Close" on the upper right to return to the desktop.

If the sound library already has your material, this empty state entry may not appear; you can select an existing material to practice, and do not clear your project to match the screenshot.

2. Click on the kick drum and snare drum to identify the loaded tracks.

Tap the "Kick Drum" and "Snare Drum" drum pads that have loaded preset sounds successively. The speakers will play crisp percussion sound effects, and the small screen will simultaneously highlight the name of the currently triggered track.

Empty drum pads are in a state to be assigned, which is convenient for subsequent recording or importing custom audio; it cannot be considered that the audio has been loaded just because the grid exists, please look for the drum pads marked with names.

3. Open the sampling menu and enter sound connection and fine tuning.

Click the sampling menu entry on the small screen above and select "Sound Connection and Fine Tuning". Double-check the name of the sound you're currently editing at the top of the edit page to make sure it's the one you want to fine-tune.

If you select the wrong sound, return to the desktop and re-select the target drum pad. The sampling menu also provides rich access to audition, recording collection and slice production. This exercise first focuses on the attribute calibration of a single audio track.

4. Listen to the sound, fine-tune the gain and experience loudness sorting

Click "Audio Sound" on the sound editing page, drag the volume slider to make small changes, and listen again to compare the loudness. For sample clips with lower recording levels, you can click "Organize Loudness" to adjust the loudness, and then listen to the changes.

Loudness sorting should be used flexibly according to the listening sense of the material to avoid pushing all parameters to the maximum at one time, causing audio overload distortion. If necessary, you can use the undo button to restore the settings.

5. Configure sound connection: distinguish between overlay playback and relay playback

In the "Sound Connection" settings, check the "Overlay Play" and "Relay Play" options. Overlay playback allows multiple pad sounds to sound mixed together at the same time; relay playback (mutually exclusive group) will immediately cut off the tail of the previous sound when a new sound is triggered.

After selecting the relay mode, return to the drum pads and alternately hit the two drum pads in the same group to hear the effect. If two sounds are not classified into the same mutually exclusive group, they will not automatically mute each other. Please check the mutually exclusive group assignments on the fine-tuning page.

6. Import custom materials and create slices

After mastering sound editing, you can return to the sound recording desktop and click "Import File", or record a short piece of sound from the collection portal. After new material enters the sound library, still follow the sequence of "Select → Audition → Check Name → Adjust".

When you want to slice, enter "Cut into several small sounds" from the menu, listen to the boundaries first, and then save the slice. The exported project ZIP is used for backup; there is currently no in-app project package import interface, so do not treat the backup package as a project format that can be directly re-imported.

FAQ

No sound from drum pad?

First make sure it has sound bound, then check the device volume, audio output and whether it is still recording. Playback will be paused during microphone collection; sound needs to be added to empty cells first.

Why are some cells empty?

Presets allow continued addition of sounds. The four samples only occupy part of the space, and the empty space does not mean that the entire sampler is unavailable, nor does it need to be forced to fill up to play.

What is the relationship between slices and full soundtracks?

Slices record the playback range in the original sound, and the original file is retained. Listen to the slice boundaries before saving; don't interpret on-screen dividing lines to mean that the original audio has been deleted.
