Introduction to guitar: strum the six strings and follow the prompts to practice a chord

Get to know the strings, chords and consoles of the guitar desktop, then enter the learning desktop to select songs, listen to demonstrations and practice step by step.

September 11, 2026

Start by strumming one chord, then learn to move to the next one when the time is right. NinoPad puts six inductive strings and chord rockers on your mobile phone, and also provides a learning desktop. This article starts with the simplest strumming and leads you to a harmony that can be practiced repeatedly.

Picture description:The pictures are from the native app in the dedicated simulator, and are actually operated and screenshotted by the script. The connection state uses demo data; the local operation in this article does not require a Mac connection. Screenshots do not serve as verification of actual Mac MIDI output.

Before starting

  • Update to Naimao Magic Control 1.3.0, select "Guitar" in "Layout".
  • Practice with the default scheme first, turn down the volume, and confirm that the sound is playing from the intended speakers or headphones.
  • No need to connect to a Mac to play and learn locally; lessons use standard tunings and fixed fingerings.

1. Strum the strings first and hear the current chord clearly.

Once on the guitar tabletop, lightly sweep across the six strings. You can tap on each string one by one, or you can strum multiple strings in succession; start slowly and make sure that each touch has a corresponding sound.

The direction of the strings can be adjusted. Regardless of whether it is arranged horizontally or vertically, swipe in the direction that can cross each string in turn. There is no need to copy the gesture direction on the screenshot. The small screen above displays the currently selected chord.

2. Change the chords and try strumming again

Use the eight-way rocker to select another direction, first look at the chord name on the small screen, and then strum the strings. First divide "chord selection" and "strumming" into two actions, and then connect them together after you are familiar with them.

Use the mute key when you want to stop the sound immediately; the bend bar is used to change the pitch when the sound is made, and it will return to the center when you release it. There is no need to operate these controls simultaneously the first time, start by switching between the two chords steadily.

3. Understand sounds and solutions from the console

Click "Control" and you can see the entrances of "Sound", "Performance", "Chords" and "Plan". First understand the current sound from the sound page, and then check your own chords and plans to avoid thinking of tuning and changing chords as the same thing.

Modifications in the console are saved to the current scenario. If you want to keep the original configuration, make a copy on the plan page and try again. After clicking "Finish" to return to the playing desktop, use the same chord to compare the sound before and after the adjustment.

4. Switch to the learning desktop and choose a song to practice

Enter "Learning" in the desktop switch bar at the bottom, and click on the small screen above to open the song selection page. You can search by title or practice technique, or filter by style and difficulty; the full list has 100 exercises.

This time, enter "Jasmine" in the search box and click on the search result. The contents of the music library are independently arranged and simplified short harmony exercises, not original recordings or complete song scores. Choose a name that you are familiar with first to help you remember the chord changes you want to practice.

5. Listen to the demonstration first and observe the changes in prompts

After selecting the song, return to the learning desktop, keep the "Demonstration" mode first, and click play. Observe the chords, bars, and progressions on the small screen, and then pay attention to the prompts on the strings and joysticks. If you want to watch a section again, you can pause and adjust the progress.

Listen to a complete round for the first time, don't rush to catch up on all the prompts. Learn to use the course's own tunings and fingerings. The capo, transposition, or custom open strings you set in the playing plan will not be directly applied.

6. Cut to follow step by step and complete the movements slowly

After pausing the demonstration, switch the mode to "Follow Practice" and start practicing step by step. Follow the current chord and string position prompts and complete the current action correctly before proceeding to the next step. A strum will only advance the step corresponding to the first touch, and the entire exercise will not be skipped just because you drag through multiple strings.

If you encounter unfamiliar finger shapes, you can click on the small screen or the console to view them; enter the practice settings when you need to slow down and set the loop. When finished, click Pause, then change the song or return to the playing table, so that you can easily know where to continue next time.

FAQ

Why don't you use my capo when studying?

The course uses standard tuning and fixed finger patterns, making it easy to practice according to the prompts. Capos, transpositions, and custom tunings for strumming plans do not directly override lessons.

The console does not turn on when strumming. Is it malfunctioning?

This prevents accidental opening of the console while the strings are in contact and for a short time after they are released. First let go and pause, then tap the console in place; starting to slide from the button may also be recognized as a strum gesture and cancel the opening.

Can lessons replace real guitar fingering practice?

The mobile phone mainly helps to understand the chord sequence, string position and rhythm. The fret strength, hand shape, and feel of a real guitar still require practice on the instrument.
