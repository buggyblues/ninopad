From installation to connection, just three steps

No complicated network configuration required, just swipe to connect on the same LAN Wi-Fi

STEP 01

Install Mac version

Download the DMG image from the official Releases, drag "NinoPad Magic Control" into the "Applications" folder and start it.

STEP 02

Enable system permissions

Turn on the "Accessibility" permission in "System Settings → Privacy and Security"; if you need a desktop screen preview, then turn on the "Screen Recording" permission.

STEP 03

Scan the QR code to connect with one click

Make sure your iPhone/iPad and Mac are connected to the same Wi-Fi, open the mobile terminal and scan the QR code to pair successfully in seconds.

Frequently Asked Support Questions Handbook

Answer common questions about network connection, permission troubleshooting, membership subscription and privacy security

Where to download the official version for iPhone, iPad and Mac?

iPhone / iPad please go to Download NinoPad Magic Control from App Store, no need for TestFlight. Mac pleaseDownload the supporting terminal, drag the app into "Applications" and open it. After the installation is complete, connect both ends to the same LAN, and then use the mobile app to scan the Mac's pairing QR code.

Why does my phone time out when scanning the QR code on my Mac?

Please first make sure your phone and Mac are inSame Wi-Fi network(Do not connect one to 5G Wi-Fi and the other to guest Wi-Fi), and the "AP isolation" function is not turned on on the router. If the network is normal and you still can't connect, you can right-click on the Mac menu bar to exit completely and try again.

How to troubleshoot if the keys suddenly fail after upgrading the macOS system?

Accessibility permissions of old signatures may remain after a major macOS version update. Please go to "System Settings → Privacy and Security → Accessibility", select "NinoPad" and click the minus sign below - Delete and click the plus sign + Add it again to return to normal.

Why can’t I see the real-time view of my Mac desktop on my mobile phone?

Real-time preview of desktop screen needs to be granted separately"Screen recording" permission. Please turn on NinoPad Magic Control in "System Settings → Privacy and Security → Screen Recording". Regular keys, knobs, and trackpads are not affected by this permission.

How to restore Pro membership after changing to a new phone or reinstalling?

Go to "Settings → Milkcat Pro" on your iPhone or iPad and click"Restore Purchase"That’s it. All subscriptions and purchases are hosted by the Apple App Store and can be seamlessly restored as long as you log in with the same Apple ID. No secondary purchases are required on the Mac.

Will the operation data and images be uploaded to the cloud?

Never upload. All key commands, text input content and screen real-time images,100% direct point-to-point transmission within local LAN Wi-Fi only, the official server does not relay or store any operational data, fully ensuring privacy and engineering security.

What Mac hardware and macOS system versions are supported?

Supporting all Mac computers running macOS 13 (Ventura) and higher, the Universal architecture is natively compatible with Apple Silicon (M1/M2/M3/M4 series chips) and Intel processors.

Still need further assistance?

Encountered an unusual bug or have new feature suggestions? You are welcome to submit a ticket on GitHub Issues. It is recommended to include your macOS and iOS versions and reproduction steps.
