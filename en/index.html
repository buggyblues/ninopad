FAQ

Answers to frequently asked questions about LAN connections, permission troubleshooting, membership subscriptions, and privacy security

View the full help and support manual →
Do my devices need to be on the same Wi-Fi network?

Remote control requires your iPhone or iPad and Mac to share a local network. Control traffic stays on that network; an internet connection is not required for basic control.

Why can't my Mac receive control commands or screens?

Regular keys and controls need to be allowed for NinoPad in Mac's "System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility"; if real-time screen mirroring is required, the "Screen Recording" permission needs to be turned on separately.

Are basic functions free? How to purchase and restore Pro membership?

Core basic keypad and system controls are completely free. Pro members can go to "Settings → NinoPad Pro" on iPhone/iPad to subscribe or buy out. All transactions are processed by the Apple App Store, and one-click recovery of purchases is supported.

Will control instructions and data be uploaded to the cloud?

Mac controls, text input and screen images travel directly between paired devices on the local network, without a cloud relay. Optional iCloud sync and third-party services have separate data flows; see the privacy policy.

What Mac devices and systems are supported?

Supports Mac devices running macOS 13 (Ventura) and higher, and the Universal architecture is natively compatible with Apple Silicon (M series chips) and Intel processors.
