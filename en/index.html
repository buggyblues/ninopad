Do iPhone/iPad and Mac need to be on the same Wi-Fi?

Yes. NinoPad communicates directly over your local Wi-Fi network, requiring no internet or cloud relay for minimal latency and maximum privacy.

Why is Mac not responding to key presses or screen stream?

Basic hotkeys and cursor control require Accessibility permission in macOS 'System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility'. Screen mirroring requires 'Screen Recording' permission.

Are core features free? How do I purchase or restore Pro?

All core keypads, trackpad, and system controls are 100% free. Pro membership can be subscribed to or purchased as a lifetime unlock inside the iOS app via Apple StoreKit, with full support for restoring purchases across devices.

Is my data or screen content uploaded to the cloud?

Never. All control commands, text, and screen streams are transmitted strictly peer-to-peer within your local area network without passing through any external servers.

Which Mac devices and operating systems are supported?

Supports macOS 13 (Ventura) and later. The Universal binary natively supports both Apple Silicon (M1/M2/M3/M4 chips) and Intel-based Macs.
